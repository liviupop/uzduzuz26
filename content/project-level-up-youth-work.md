March 2026. An Erasmus+ professional-development programme in Paphos, hosted by [CCIF Cyprus](partner-ccif), bringing together youth workers and organisations from across Europe.

## what it was

*Level Up: Innovate, Inspire & Empower Youth Work* was a space for comparing practice rather than simply presenting organisations. Participants worked through questions of non-formal education, learning mobility, empowerment, facilitation, and the language youth workers use when they describe change.

uzinaduzina joined as a beneficiary organisation, continuing the Cluj–Paphos working relationship established through Green Genesis Startup and strengthened through several later mobilities.

## what we contributed

Radu and Andreea Pop delivered a practical session on photographing youth work professionally and painlessly with a phone. The workshop treated documentation as part of the educational process: useful evidence, a form of care for participants, and a way to communicate without interrupting the activity being documented.

Diana Spîrleanu accompanied the group through the programme, bringing the facilitation and wellbeing practice she has developed across uzinaduzina's youth exchanges.

## why it matters

Professional development becomes useful when it moves in both directions. We went to Paphos to learn from the international group, but also brought back a tested workshop format that turns visual documentation into a realistic skill for small youth organisations.
