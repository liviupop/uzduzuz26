April 2026. A study visit in Trogir, Croatia, organised by [CCIF Cyprus](partner-ccif) and Udruga Murtila around a direct question: how can local youth work connect to European opportunities without losing its local intelligence?

## what it was

*Stronger Together: Connecting Local Youth Work to European Opportunities* brought organisations together for study visits, exchange of practice, networking, and the early design of future cooperation.

uzinaduzina joined as a beneficiary organisation. The programme allowed us to reconnect with trusted partners from Green Genesis Startup while meeting organisations whose work starts from different local conditions.

## why it matters

European partnerships often begin before an application form exists. They begin by seeing how another organisation works, what it can genuinely carry, and whether the conversation remains useful when there is no immediate deadline.

For uzinaduzina, Trogir was a consolidation visit: strengthening the Erasmus+ ecosystem around relationships already tested in practice, while keeping space open for new project ideas.
