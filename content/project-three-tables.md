An oral-history and food archive built around everyday recipes: what people cooked yesterday, what can be cooked without gas or electricity, what travels with them when they leave, and what must be replaced.

## what it is

*Three Tables / Trei mese / Три трапези* is a 2026 cross-border cultural project connecting field research in Budjak and the Odesa region with an archive residency in Tulcea. It looks at food not as a catalogue of festive dishes, but as a way into memory, adaptation, displacement, and ordinary resilience.

The project is implemented within **ZMINA:Resilience**, a cooperation between IZOLYATSIA (Ukraine) and Malý Berlín (Slovakia), with support from Trans Europe Halles (Sweden), and is co-funded by the European Union.

## how the fieldwork works

Before the first field trip, uzinaduzina worked online with the DOBRO youth centre and Odesa WOW to read the field guide page by page. The guide covers both technique and care:

- asking to sit in the kitchen rather than the formal room;
- recording so that the speaker's voice remains clear;
- recognising questions that open a memory and questions that close it;
- requesting permission, on the recording and on paper, before material becomes public;
- allowing silence rather than completing another person's sentence;
- stopping the recording when pain appears.

The field guide is deliberately provisional. The field will correct it; the revised version becomes an open methodology published at the end of the project.

## what happens next

Field research takes place in Budjak, Frumushika-Nova, and the wider Odesa region with DOBRO and Odesa WOW. Recordings, photographs, recipes, and field notes then travel to Tulcea, where an October residency turns them into the first entries of the archive.

> One recipe with a good story is worth more than ten without one.
